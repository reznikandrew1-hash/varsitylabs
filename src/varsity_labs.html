How every lot earns its label

Four steps, in order, before a vial can be sold. Scroll through them.

  1. 01

    Filled and numbered

    A lot is one production run. Every vial from it carries that run's number and fill date, so a report always points at the exact vials you hold.

  2. 02

    Sent to the lab

    Before labels, samples go to an independent laboratory for HPLC purity, mass-spec identity, endotoxin, sterility and heavy metals. We don't choose the numbers.

  3. 03

    Published, pass or hold

    The certificate goes on the reports page the day it's signed. A pass ships. Anything else stays on hold where everyone can see it.

  4. 04

    Packed cold

    Insulated box, ice, temperature strip, plain exterior. The QR on the label opens the report you just read about.

Not for people or animals

Products are not for human or veterinary use, not for in vivo use in humans or animals, and not for diagnostic, therapeutic or clinical procedures. Who can buy

Orders are accepted only from buyers who are at least 21 and confirm they are qualified researchers, or are purchasing on behalf of a research institution or entity, for in vitro use. We may ask for verification and may refuse or cancel any order.

Information on this site

Product pages describe identity, composition, purity, handling and storage. They are educational. Lab reports document what is in the vial, not what it does. Our reps and support team do not give handling, dosing or use guidance of any kind.
