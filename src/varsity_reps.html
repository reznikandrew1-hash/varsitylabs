Questions

Do I need a website or a big following?No. Most of our best reps have under 2,000 followers and a very active group chat.
When do I get paid?Monthly. Statement on the 1st for the previous month, paid by the 15th, on delivered orders only, $50 minimum (it rolls over). A 30-day hold on first orders covers refunds.
Can I use my own code?No, and we check. Codes are for other people's orders. You get the product allowance instead.
What if a customer forgets my code?Your link sets a 30-day cookie, so the order still counts. If both fail, email us within 7 days with the order number and we'll fix it.
Can I be a rep at a school that isn't listed?Yes. Pick "Other Florida college" and tell us the school in the form. New campuses get a manager once there are four active reps.
What can I post?The unboxing, the QR scan to the report, the cold pack, the delivery. What we test, not what it does. The posting guide has templates.
